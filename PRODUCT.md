# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

1. **School Administrators & Operations Staff**: Managing student information system (SIS), tuition fee schedules, route planning, bus fleet dispatch, and institutional reports.
2. **Parents & Guardians**: Tracking student attendance, checking homework and grades, settling tuition and bus fees via Bakong KHQR, and monitoring bus GPS location in real time.
3. **Classroom Teachers**: Conducting morning homeroom roll calls with one-tap bus auto-sync, publishing grades, and posting classroom bulletins.
4. **Bus Drivers & Assistants**: Viewing route stop sequence manifests, student boarding checklist, NFC/QR scan check-in, and broadcasting delay notices.
5. **Students (Grade 6–12)**: Accessing daily class timetable, homework deadlines, digital ID card, and bus arrival countdowns.

## Product Purpose

A unified School Operating System (School OS) and Transport Safety Platform designed specifically for Cambodian private and international schools. The system eliminates fragmented paper registers, manual cash collections, and uncoordinated school bus transport by unifying SIS, Bakong KHQR payments, and live transit safety.

## Positioning

The only school management platform offering native synchronization between fleet transit check-in, classroom homeroom attendance roll call, and National Bank of Cambodia (NBC) Bakong KHQR payment reconciliation.

## Operating Context

- Bilingual English and Khmer (`ភាសាខ្មែរ`) typography.
- Mobile PWA device simulation for Parents, Students, Teachers, and Bus Staff.
- High-density desktop operations cockpit for School Administrators.
- Urban traffic realities of Phnom Penh (advisories, delays, route re-ordering).

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **Core Ecosystem**: 5 Daily Operational Apps (Admin Portal, Parent App, Student App, Teacher App, Bus Staff App).
- **Payment Gateway**: Bakong KHQR direct settlement and dynamic QR code generation.
- **Transport Safety**: Live vehicle telemetry simulation, stop checklists, QR/NFC boarding verification, parent geofence notifications.
- **Attendance**: Homeroom physical roll call with one-tap automated roll syncing from bus onboarding data.

## Brand Commitments

- **Tone**: Quiet, authoritative, refined institutional craft.
- **Visuals**: Zero "AI slop" or saturated gradient noise; high-contrast readability, clean neutral containers, and purposeful functional semantic colors.
- **Typography**: Inter paired with Noto Sans Khmer for native linguistic balance.

## Product Principles

1. **Safety First**: Bus telemetry and boarding status must be findable in seconds without cognitive overload.
2. **Calm Operations**: Restrained color palette (neutral dominance with 10% semantic accents) to keep teachers and administrators focused.
3. **Local Truth**: Built for Cambodian payment rails (Bakong KHQR) and dual-language comprehension.
